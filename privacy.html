RigenFarms Privacy Policy

Clear, practical information about privacy and data use in our gardening community.

Last updated: Feb 2026

1) Information We Collect

We may collect information when you make a donation, register as a volunteer, submit a contact form, or subscribe to our newsletter. Depending on your interaction, we may collect your name, contact details, optional profile information, donation references, and basic technical data for security and analytics.

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless you choose to provide it for a specific purpose.

2) How We Use Personal Information

Your information helps us process donations, respond to inquiries, send occasional updates and impact stories, improve website performance, and understand general engagement without profiling individuals.

3) Sharing & Disclosure

RigenFarms treats your information like a precious seed — we don't sell, rent, or trade it. We may share data only with payment providers, trusted service partners, or if required by law.

4) Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files that help our website remember your preferences. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

5) Payments & Security

We use secure, recognized payment channels. We do not store full card or mobile money PINs on our servers. Transaction references are stored only for verification and accounting.

6) Third-Party Services

Our website may link to or embed content from third parties. These services may collect data according to their own policies. RigenFarms is not responsible for their practices.

7) Your Rights & Data Requests

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to unsubscribe from communications at any time. Contact us using the details below.

8) Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as our gardens grow and our practices evolve. The "Last updated" date will always reflect the latest version.
