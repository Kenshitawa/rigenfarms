Our Climate-Smart Programs

Rooted in education and environmental stewardship, we equip schools and communities with innovative wicking container gardens to improve food security, nutrition, and resilience against climate change.

Home•Programs

Cultivating Resilient Communities

Through hands-on training, school partnerships, and community-led initiatives, we transform how people grow food — one wicking bed at a time.

Flagship

School Wicking Garden Program

We partner with primary and secondary schools to install wicking container gardens that serve as living laboratories. Students learn sustainable agriculture while producing fresh vegetables for school meals.

Wicking Bed Technology & Training

Our core innovation — wicking container gardens — uses up to 50% less water and boosts yields. We provide DIY workshops, starter kits, and technical support for households and institutions.
