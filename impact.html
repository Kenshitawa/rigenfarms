How We Measure Impact

RigenFarms tracks outcomes before, during, and after each growing season, combining community feedback with field data and follow‑up visits.

Our Evidence Approach

  • Pre‑ and post‑training assessments with participants
  • Garden growth logs and harvest records
  • Water usage tracking in wicking beds vs. conventional plots
  • School feeding programme data and attendance
  • Community validation meetings and story collection

Common Outcome Areas

  • Improved water efficiency and crop yields
  • Increased access to fresh vegetables for families
  • Strengthened school nutrition and pupil concentration
  • Growth of youth‑led agribusiness initiatives
  • Enhanced community resilience to climate shocks
