About RigenFarms

Rooted in education and environmental stewardship, RigenFarms empowers schools and communities with wicking container gardening and climate‑smart farming, improving food security, nutrition, and resilience against climate change.

Our Story

From a Simple Idea to a Growing Movement

RigenFarms began in 2022 when a group of young agricultural enthusiasts saw the potential of wicking container gardens to transform school feeding and household nutrition in water‑scarce communities. What started as a pilot in a single school has grown into a community‑driven initiative reaching thousands across multiple counties.

We don't just install gardens — we train teachers, students, and families to build their own climate‑smart gardens, turning barren spaces into productive, water‑efficient food sources that improve diets and build resilience.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Approach

Vision

A Kenya where every school and community grows its own food using climate‑smart, water‑wise techniques — nourished, resilient, and ecologically aware.

Mission

To empower schools and communities with innovative wicking container gardening and sustainable farming methods that improve food security and build climate resilience.

Approach

We work hands‑on with farmers, teachers, and youth — providing training, starter kits, and continuous mentorship to ensure gardens thrive and knowledge spreads.

Our Core Values

The Values That Guide Us

Sustainability

Programs designed to be self‑sustaining, using locally available materials and knowledge that multiplies.

Community‑Driven

Communities lead the change; we facilitate, train, and provide tools for lasting food security.

Collaboration

Partnering with schools, county governments, NGOs, and research institutions to scale impact.

Innovation

Wicking container gardening, climate‑smart techniques, and youth‑led solutions for modern agriculture.

Integrity

Transparent and honest in our operations, from seed distribution to impact reporting.

Inclusivity

Women, youth, and persons with disabilities are central to our programs and decision‑making.

Empowerment

Knowledge transfer that builds confidence and turns recipients into trainers and community leaders.

Water Stewardship

Responsible water use is at the heart of our wicking technology and farming training.
